# Suggest or correct a grant

[Open an issue](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/nonprofit-grants/issues/new) with the funder's name, the link to
their own page, the deadline and who can apply. Corrections are welcome: say which row is wrong and link the
funder's page showing the right information.

Please do not post personal information, application drafts or anything confidential in an issue.
This list is a set of leads. It does not apply for anything for anyone, and it is not legal or financial advice.
